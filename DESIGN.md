# OnlineStore visual direction

The user-pinned PDF catalog is the visual authority. Storefront is an Operate surface with a welcoming banner; administration remains a dense, legible Operate surface. No alternative concept round or publishing is needed for this explicit local brief.

Deep ink navigation, mint banner, emerald primary actions, warm white page, pale neutral image stages. Product images have space to breathe. A left category rail and responsive grid echo the PDF. The home banner leads to actual products, without invented sales or shipping claims. Admin uses a sidebar, horizontal table overflow and focused native dialogs.

Typography: locally served Noto Sans Georgian where available, readable system fallback. Tokens live in css/styles.css. Clear focus outlines, labeled controls, 44px targets, reduced-motion support. No ratings or discounts are invented. Local SVG product placeholders are illustrative geometry, not product photographs. Source: original project-authored assets; no external image URLs.
